# Legal Notes

ECHO: GalacticCore is an unofficial ECHO Platform port/fork of Galacticraft Legacy by TeamGalacticraft.

The project preserves the original MIT license and credit. Public-facing copy should use the wording below:

- ECHO: GalacticCore
- Unofficial ECHO Platform port/fork of Galacticraft Legacy.
- Derived from Galacticraft Legacy by TeamGalacticraft under the MIT License.

Release-facing copy is guarded by `:echogalacticcore:verifyIdentityLegal`.

Avoid wording that implies official continuity, endorsement, or replacement.

- Do not say "Official Galacticraft".
- Do not say "Galacticraft 6".
- Do not say "Galacticraft for ECHO".
- Do not say "The new Galacticraft".
- Do not say "Made by TeamGalacticraft".

Runtime ids should use the `echogalacticcore` namespace. Legacy `galacticraftcore` and `galacticraftplanets` ids are source references only unless a future migration tool explicitly maps old saves.
