# Deep Reach Design Summary

## Experience pitch

Deep Reach is a pressure-suit survival experience. Players descend from flooded surface entry points into a vertical world of ancient ruins, volcanic rifts, and bioluminescent abyssal ecosystems. The loop is built around four pressures: oxygen, depth, light, and suit integrity.

## Depth zones

| Zone | Depth | Identity | Key pressure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shallows | 0-64 | Sunlit flooded caves and sunken roads | Oxygen discipline |
| Twilight Trench | 64-128 | Bioluminescent forests and wreckage | Light / predator exposure |
| Abyssal Plain | 128-192 | Crushing dark desert of silt and ruins | Depth pressure |
| Geothermal Rifts | 192+ | Magma-heated vents and molten pockets | Heat + suit integrity |

## Suits

- **Dive Suit (starter)**: Short oxygen, no pressure resistance.
- **Pressure Suit**: Moderate depth tolerance, upgradeable seals.
- **Abyssal Exosuit**: Heavy depth/heat resistance, powered by energy cells.
- **Salvage Rig**: Cargo-focused, slower movement, high tool integration.

## Habitats

- Compact deployable habitats restore oxygen and suit integrity.
- Modules add crafting, mapping, storage, and vehicle docking.
- Power comes from surface-pumped cables, geothermal taps, or portable fuel cells.

## Vehicles

- **Mini-Sub**: Two-seat personal transport with cargo rack.
- **Drill Crawler**: Slow, armored, can carve tunnels and withstand pressure.
- **Glider Frame**: Vertical ascent/descent harness for narrow shafts.

## Campaign

1. **Descent**: Establish first habitat and oxygen pipeline.
2. **Survey**: Map ruins, locate precursor signal sources.
3. **Survive**: Manage suit upgrades, pressure, and hostile fauna.
4. **Reach**: Activate the deep relay and unlock endgame expeditions.
